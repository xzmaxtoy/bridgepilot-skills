# Topic ideation (short form)

Goal: 3-5 post ideas the client can film or already has footage for. Not generic ideas; each one ends in a concrete shot list and a comment hook.

## Inputs
The profile (services, area, voice, differentiators), the jobs the client has coming up or has footage for, last week's winners (`insights.md`), the season.

## Signals (use at least two)
1. Platform: what same-topic posts with high saves look like (hook, structure, length). Optional, untested as a routine; one scan, a few posts, never the client's own.
2. Customers: the questions in comments and DMs (`inbox-triage.md` class C and G), what they worry about.
3. Account: topics already posted, what is a good series.

## Method
1. Frame the topic: object (a yard, a material, a step), action (before/after, how it is done, a mistake to avoid), emotion (satisfying, relief, surprise), benefit (save time, avoid a mistake).
2. Three angles per topic: the process (how it is done), the result (what changed), the decision (how to choose or what to watch for).
3. Title skeletons (adapt, do not copy): 为什么___ / 我们发现___ / ___到底值不值 / 别再___了 / 做完___之后.
4. Add one comment hook per idea: a question a homeowner can answer in one line.

## Filter (each idea must pass)
Shootable with the footage the client has or can get this week; one clear shot sequence (before, during, after); no claim the profile cannot source; no customer who has not agreed; not a repeat of the last two posts.

## Output per idea
Title (20 characters at most), angle, target viewer, comment hook, the shot list (3 shots), the claim list (all sourced), risk note. Label all Chinese DRAFT. If signals are too thin, output a list of questions for the client instead of inventing "viral" ideas.
